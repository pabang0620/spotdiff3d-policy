Privacy Policy

nekolab (the "Company") provides the mobile game "3D Spot the Difference: Cozy" (Korean title 「3D 틀린그림찾기: 포근한 방」, the "Game") and handles users' personal information as described below, in line with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea.

1. Information the Company collects directly

The Game has no sign-up or login and does not send user information to any server run by the Company. The Company does not collect names, dates of birth, phone numbers, addresses, email addresses or location information.

If you email us with a question, we use your email address and message only as needed to reply, and delete them within one year after replying.

2. Information stored only on your device

Game progress (cleared stages, stars, best times, hints) and settings (sound, vibration, language, pinned music) are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the Game.
